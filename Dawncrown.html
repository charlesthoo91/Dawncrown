⚔ Dawncrown v0.7

First to 3 points wins. You score a point each time you defeat the enemy character.

Field (per player)

  • One character at a time, plus one Equipment slot and one Magic slot.
  • Characters start as Rookie (R) and job-change to Elite (E) then Champion (C). Equipment gives XP; enough XP triggers the job change.
  • Strong, Medium and Weak attacks. Weak hits lightly but has a special effect.

Every round

  • ① Preparation: each player in turn draws to 5 and plays 1 card (or passes).
  • ② Battle: both players secretly pick an attack. Step 1 — Weak effects: every Weak attack's special effect (Parry, Counter, Weaken, Heal…) takes effect before any damage. Step 2 — Strikes: the characters hit one after the other. A character defeated by the first strike cannot strike back, so there are no ties.
  • Initiative ⚡ alternates each round. Whoever has it prepares first and strikes first in step 2. The other player prepares second and sees the opponent's cards.
  • Face-down deployment: the first player's newly deployed character is hidden (and can't be targeted) until the second player deploys theirs — or until the battle starts.
  • A battle only happens if both sides have a character.
  • After Strong, it needs 1 round to recover. Stances last until the end of the next round.
  • No single hit can take more than 50% of max HP. From round 15 attacks gain bonus damage.

Feeding XP

  • Besides deploying, a character card in your hand can be played onto your own character on the field to give it XP: +1 XP for a different job, +2 XP for the same job. The card is used up.

Controls

  • Tap a hand card, then a glowing slot, then Done. Tap a character to inspect it. 📖 shows every job.
